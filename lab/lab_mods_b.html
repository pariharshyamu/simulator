SIM-6 · Performance & efficiency

Combustion multi-objective optimiser

Koradi Units 8-10 ran a gross heat rate of 2,272 kcal/kWh against a normative 2,096 — a 176 kcal/kWh gap on 964.8 MU, the single largest thermodynamic loss in the fleet in June 2026. Combustion optimisation is the most established closed-loop AI application in thermal generation, and also the one that needs the most engineering around it.

The problem. There is no single best combustion setting. Lowering excess O₂ improves heat rate and lowers NOx, but raises unburnt carbon and CO and can destabilise the flame. Raising over-fire air lowers NOx but raises unburnt carbon. It is a trade-off surface, and where you choose to sit on it is a management decision about the relative price of fuel, of a NOx penalty and of ash saleability — not a technical one.

SIM-7 · Generative AI

RAG — grounding AI in your own documents

Retrieval-augmented generation is the difference between a party trick and a plant tool. This module runs a real retrieval engine over an indexed corpus of plant documents and shows you exactly what the model would be given before it answers.

The problem. A public chatbot answers from what it half-remembers of the internet. It has never read your OEM manual, your SOPs or your trip reports — and it will not tell you that. It will produce a clearance figure with total confidence and no source. RAG changes the order of operations: search your documents first, then answer only from what was found, and cite it.

The decomposition — auxiliary power against boiler and turbine

Gross heat rate = net heat rate × (1 − auxiliary fraction). A negative gross gap means the boiler and turbine are beating the norm and the whole penalty is auxiliary power.

Fuel economics, derived from each station's own merit-order variable charge

Cost of heat = MOD variable charge ÷ actual net heat rate. Implied as-fired coal cost = cost of heat × as-fired GCV. This is the station's own money, not an assumption.

Value at stake, June 2026

Do not add columns 2 and 4. Net heat rate already contains auxiliary power; the auxiliary figure is a component of the heat-rate figure, not an addition to it.

What this data does not tell you. One month is not a trend, and June is a low-demand-season month. Normative values are regulatory constructs, not thermodynamic optima. The filing does not separate planned overhaul from forced outage inside the availability figure. Heat rate is a monthly average across a group of units, so a single poor unit hides inside a group number. And auxiliary consumption is structurally higher at low PLF, so part of the auxiliary gap at Chandrapur 3-7 and Khaperkheda 1-4 is a consequence of being backed down rather than of inefficiency — separating those two is itself a worthwhile analytics project.
